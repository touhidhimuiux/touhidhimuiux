## 🧪 Research & Projects

### 1. Visual Robustness and Adversarial Attacks on Vision-Language Models

**Research Project | Undergraduate Research**

Investigating how visual perturbations affect the performance and internal representations of vision-language models on chart-based visual question answering tasks.

**Research objectives:**
- Evaluate visual attacks on chart understanding models.
- Investigate image corruption and adversarial perturbations.
- Compare clean and attacked model predictions.
- Analyze layer-wise representation changes.
- Study the robustness of different vision-language architectures.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, Vision-Language Models, Deep Learning, Computer Vision.

**Status:** Ongoing research project.
